05 · AI Study Pal

A study partner that shows its sources.

Checking whether Study Pal is switched on for this site…

What it can do

  • Explain a topic step by step, at your level
  • Answer from notes you choose, and point to the page or section used
  • Turn notes into a practice quiz you can save to Practice
  • Give coding hints and help with a revision plan

What to know

  • It needs a GradePath account and an AI provider set up on the server
  • Free accounts have a small daily allowance; Plus is planned at ₦1,000 a month
  • It says when your notes don't answer the question
  • Conversations are saved only if you choose to save them on your device

06 · New · Nigerian History

Nigeria's story, told with its evidence.

From Nok terracottas to the return of civilian rule, each story says what it rests on: excavated objects, written records or oral tradition. Where historians disagree, you see both sides and the sources.

Explore Nigerian History →

The first 12 stories are drafts written from published histories and are waiting for a historian's review. Each one is labelled.

  1. c. 900 BCENok terracottas
  2. 9th–10th c.Igbo-Ukwu bronzes
  3. 1804Sokoto Caliphate
  4. 1929The Women's War
  5. 1960Independence

Nigerian languages: courses in Hausa, Igbo and Yorùbá are planned first. None are open yet. A language opens only after native speakers have checked its lessons, spelling and tone marks.
